# Morning report: Stalingrad (interim, written 2026-11-09 15:00 UTC)

This is an early version. The work is still running; the final report replaces this file when it is done.

**Stalingrad is not live and has not been pushed anywhere.** The live game is still Beta v0.12.0. Stalingrad waits on its
branches until you say "push to live".

## Done so far

- **Units unlocked by missions again (live as v0.12.0 last night).** Medals buy upgrades (10, 20, 30 medals for levels 1
  to 3) and the plane call-ins (210, 300, 450). Medals spent on units were refunded. A first campaign run earns about
  250 medals.
- **Soviet units and uniforms:** soldiers with the SSh-40 helmet, pilotka, ushanka, greatcoat roll, quilted jacket and
  snow smock; Mosin, PPSh, PPD, DP-27, PTRD, Maxim, TT-33; T-34, KV-1, KV-2, T-60, T-26, BT-7, the carrier, GAZ truck,
  Katyusha truck; Il-2, Pe-2, Yak-1, Po-2, I-16; the HQ stages and the red flag.
- **Era-correct forces by date:** each mission picks names and looks for its date. In 1941 the Germans field the early
  Panzer III and short Panzer IV; from mid-1942 the later Panzer III (long gun, spaced armour, turret bin) and the long
  Panzer IV and StuG. Romanians at Uranus wear their own uniform.
- **Music for every mission:** ten cold battle tracks, one per mission, plus the map track. Battle music is now built
  while you read the briefing, never during the battle (this was a lag cause).
- **Money** replaces Supplies in every label, with a banknote icon.
- **Prep phase** for defensive missions: build wire, foxholes, trenches and pillboxes before the enemy moves.
- **Campaign map and briefings:** Play opens the Stalingrad map with the ten missions at their real places and a front
  line that moves with the date. Every briefing has enemy and friendly intel.
- **Unlocks per mission:** 1001 sappers, riflemen, DP gunner, Tank Hunters; 1002 T-34, mortar; 1003 PPD trooper;
  1004 medic, militia; 1005 flamethrower; 1006 sniper, AA gun; 1007 lieutenant; 1009 carrier.
- **The 10 old British missions are removed**, as you asked. Saves keep medals, units, upgrades and settings.
- **New engine features the missions needed:** mission phases and objectives with side objectives and scripted events;
  long maps with ground types (snow, rubble, steppe...) and capture points you can deploy from; buildings men fight from
  floor by floor, which collapse into rubble; weather and night that limit how far units see; tank traps, minefields,
  rivers, bridges and the Volga ferry.
