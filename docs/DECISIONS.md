# Decisions made while you were asleep

Each entry: what was chosen and why. Newest at the bottom.

1. **The game lives in `tin-hats/` with its own git repo.** The folder you opened already held another game (`index.html`) and `magic-academy/`. A subfolder keeps those untouched and out of the new repo.
2. **Node, npm and git are project-local.** None of them were installed on this PC and global installs were ruled out, so portable Node 24 LTS (nodejs.org, checksum verified) and MinGit 2.56 (git-for-windows release, checksum verified) sit in `tin-hats/.tools`. The npm cache and the Playwright browser are kept there too. `x.cmd` runs any command with them on PATH. Nothing outside the project folder was changed.
3. **TypeScript 6.0 instead of 7.0.** typescript-eslint 8 only supports TypeScript below 6.1, and lint with zero warnings was required.
4. **HUD and menus are DOM elements; Phaser draws the battlefield.** DOM gives crisp text at any pixel ratio, native safe-area handling and reliable 48 px touch targets, and it keeps the Phaser 4 API surface small. The simulation stays free of both.
5. **The simulation ticks at 30 Hz and the renderer interpolates.** Animation runs at display rate, so motion is smooth while headless tests run twice as fast as a 60 Hz sim would.
6. **The damage matrix has a fifth column, `hq`.** With pillboxes nearly immune to bullets, a shared fortification column would make the rifles-only tutorial unwinnable. The HQ gets its own multipliers.
7. **Barbed wire slows only the other side's infantry.** The side that laid it knows the gaps. This stops the AI crippling its own counter-attacks.
8. **Each unit type has its own training queue, and types train in parallel.** That is what "queue count and training progress" on every button implies. Cost and population are paid when queued so the cap of 40 can never be exceeded.
9. **Sappers cannot be directly controlled.** They do not fight, and a tap on a sapper would otherwise interrupt income.
10. **Rain can happen in every theatre, including the desert.** The request was a flat 40% per map. Thunder is checked every 2 seconds at 0.5% while it rains. Weather uses its own random source and never touches gameplay.
12. **Build agents run on Sonnet, with Opus for figure animation, the renderer and visual QA.** The first parallel run on the top model used a whole 5-hour usage window in 29 minutes and every agent was cut off. Cheaper models stretch the plan's limits so more gets finished per window; work is committed at each stage so nothing is lost when a limit hits.
11. **Git commits are authored as "Tin Hats build" with your email**, set in the repo's local config only.

## Phase 1 foundations (sim, art, UI, audio)

Sim core
13. **The dump spread now mirrors for side 1.** It was a small side asymmetry in the first draft.
14. **Sim hot paths pass numbers in object fields, not as arguments.** V8 allocates for non-inlined double arguments; a fight allocated about 1.7 KB per step before this and about 25 bytes after.
15. **`SideState.trainLeft` was removed from `types.ts`.** It was internal and nothing else used it; it lives in an internal array.
16. **Mission 1 bot `firstAttackAfter` went from 85 to 105 s**, so the bot wins in about 3.1 minutes.
17. **Training keeps the leftover fraction of a tick between queued units.**
18. **Only training respects the population cap.** Mission-placed and scripted units may exceed it.
19. **The mirrored symmetry test counts decided battles over 3600 s.** About 28% of mirrored AI-vs-AI battles are still undecided after an hour; that is a Phase 4 tuning item.

Figures
20. **The figure anim stores its faction** (weapon geometry), so `layoutFigure` and `drawFigure2d` take no `look` argument.
21. **`FigureInput` gained a `victory` flag and an optional `death.dir`.**
22. **Taller figure:** legs 13.4 + 13.4 wu, head radius 5.
23. **The light rim is a separate silhouette pass** of 14 parts behind the figure.
24. **The renderer lowers figures in trenches by `VIEW.trenchDepth`;** the figure's ground is the trench floor.
25. **The pose continuity test allows 10 wu of joint movement per frame,** because the throwing hand and the blast toss are fast on purpose.
26. **Deaths are a function of time since death,** so a body stops moving at the end. A prone gunner who dies keeps most of his pose and goes limp.

Backdrops, structures, vehicles, props
27. **Italy's far layer is a steep rock massif** with a terraced abbey base (`SCENES.italy.massifHalf`). Normandy's `seaY` is 272 with larger ships. Germany's dragon's teeth are darker concrete. Holland's bridge sits on embankments over a river gap.
28. **Rain grade, night tint and ground colours stay in `THEATRES`** and are read through `getTheatreColours`; the night-tint alpha comes from `TIME_GRADES[time].tintAlpha`.
29. **The celestial backdrop layer is the one layer allowed to start above the screen** (`y` can be negative).
30. **The backdrop dev page starts `main()` after the load event,** because the sheet and seam-test modes took over 30 s and blocked page load.
31. **The flag shear and scratch screenshots stay in the dev page only.** The wrecked tank husk has a ragged turret-ring hole with embers.
32. **A single `tileSpan` tile can scale 0.5 to 1.5; from two tiles up it stays within 0.75 to 1.25.**

Effects and weather
33. **Explosion dirt-plume streaks were softened** (smaller scale, lower alpha) because the first renders looked spiky.
34. **The rain roll is always drawn,** so a debug override never shifts the weather random sequence.
35. **`forceThunder()` is reported by the next `update()` even when it is not raining.**

HUD and menus
36. **While a stick or key is held, the HUD re-sends `{type:'stick', x}` every frame** reusing one object. The app must read it at once and not store it.
37. **HUD buttons call `preventDefault` on mousedown,** so a clicked button never keeps focus and a later Space (fire) cannot re-click it.
38. **`getButtonRects()` returns true client rects,** so training buttons scrolled out of view report positions off the visible bar.
39. **The campaign map shrinks to as small as 430 px wide** so all ten pins fit on larger phones; small phones pan. The "CAMPAIGN" heading is visually hidden but kept for screen readers.
40. **The briefing objective sits above the typed orders** so it shows on 568x320.

Audio
41. **One-shot sounds are pre-rendered through `OfflineAudioContext` after `unlock()`;** until a buffer is ready the sound is built live from oscillators.
42. **The mix runs through a compressor and a soft clipper,** so output cannot pass 0.97.
43. **The audio engine never touches storage.** The app passes the saved mute setting to `createAudio({ muted })` and saves changes itself.

Integration gate
44. **`WeaponDef.sound` now names a real audio one-shot** (`rifleShot`, `mgShot`, `smgShot`, `sniperShot`, `bunkerMg`, `mortarLaunch`, `rocketLaunch`, `tankGun`, `shellWhistle`) or `'none'`. The first draft used ids like `'rifle'` that the audio engine does not have. The thrown grenade is silent at launch (`'none'`); explosions are sounded from the `explosion` event.
45. **`src/art/icons.ts` adds `paintUnitPortrait(canvas, kind, faction)`** for every unit kind. The figure module's own `paintUnitIcon` only draws stick figures, returns `false` for tanks and takes a faction, not the `(canvas, kind, side)` the HUD and menus call.
46. **Two new seam tests guard the tracks against drifting apart:** `tests/unit/art-keys.test.ts` (unique sprite keys, positive sizes, anchors in 0..1) and `tests/unit/seams.test.ts` (kinds and actions, weapon sounds and effect sprites, every key table has its sprites, the HUD factory fits the UI shell).

Renderer core (RENDER part 1)
47. **Device pixels: Scale mode NONE with game size = CSS size x dpr (dpr capped at `VIEW.maxDevicePixelRatio`) and Scale zoom 1/dpr.** Phaser 4 has no `resolution` setting; this draws at full resolution while the canvas is styled at CSS size. Camera zoom = canvas height / 540.
48. **Battlefield input uses DOM pointer events on the canvas, not Phaser input** (Phaser input is disabled). Taps, drags, flings and the wheel stay in CSS px and never see the DOM HUD above the canvas.
49. **One Phaser Layer per DEPTH slot plus one per unit row, and unit views are pooled per row.** Objects never change depth, so neither the scene nor a layer ever re-sorts; each view's images stay contiguous in its row layer. Additive glow effects get their own layer just above `DEPTH.effects`.
50. **The HQ sprite is placed so its art doorway sits on the sim's `doorX`** (the art bunker is wider than `config.hqWidth`); HQ tracers start at the art gun slit, not the sim's `gunX`.
51. **Waving flags are sheared strips: each strip Image sits in a Container and the shear is split into rotation x scale x rotation (2x2 SVD)**, because Phaser Images cannot skew. Strips are tinted by `flagStripTint`.
52. **Units get a small fixed visual x offset (`RENDER.unit.xJitter`, 7 wu) from their id,** so the four rows of one formation column do not stand in a single vertical stack. View only; tracers aim at the body as drawn.
53. **The figure rig is fed figure-space distances (moved / row scale)** so planted feet stay put at every row scale (checked: a planted ankle drifts under 0.3 wu while the body moves 10 wu).
54. **Spawn and Fall Back use a door fade**: a unit's alpha ramps over `RENDER.unit.doorFade` (26 wu) from its own HQ door, so units emerge from and vanish into the doorway.
55. **Renderer tunables live in the new `src/data/render.ts` (`RENDER`).** Shot events do not name the firing pillbox, so the renderer takes that side's pillbox nearest to the shot's x.
56. **The game loop is a fixed-step accumulator (`src/app/loop.ts`) with a 250 ms frame cut and at most 240 steps per frame;** the backlog is dropped beyond that. Every battle starts at time scale 1 (only the debug hooks change it, up to 240). Above 4x the cosmetic effects and the app's own sounds are switched off. The result delay after the battle ends (`APP.resultDelaySeconds`, 2.5) is counted in scaled battle time, so fast-forward shows the result at once, and it does not run while paused.
57. **Pause, restart, quit and mute are handled by the app, not the session** (`src/app/app.ts`). Pause stops the steps and renders with dt 0, suspends audio and shows the HUD's pause overlay (`state.paused`). The tab being hidden, a blur, pagehide or the portrait overlay pause a running battle and never resume it. Battle actions are ignored while paused. Leaving from the pause menu resumes audio so the menus have their sounds.
58. **Save data is one versioned JSON document under the key `tinhats.save` (`src/meta/save.ts`) through an injected storage.** Loading validates every field and drops what is unusable; text that is not JSON, a missing version or a version newer than this build gives a fresh save (status `corrupt`) without throwing. Upgrade points are not stored: they are `firstClearPoints` (2) per mission cleared at least once on any difficulty, so they cannot drift from the progress. A mission opens when the one before it in the list of existing missions has been cleared on any difficulty. If localStorage is unavailable the app keeps the save in memory for the session.
59. **Progress is recorded the moment the battle ends** (a win records the clear and the 2 points on a first clear), not when the result screen appears, so quitting during the short delay cannot lose it. A draw counts as a defeat. The result screen text comes from `APP.reasons` in the new `src/data/app.ts`.
60. **Supports and direct control are wired against the command API but guarded on `SIM_FEATURES`** (`src/app/session.ts`): until the sim sets `supports` or `directControl`, the HUD gets no support buttons or control card and taps on units do nothing. The sim's `control` command takes the unit's unique id. While a unit is controlled the app sends one `controlInput` per frame (and a final neutral one), and the camera follows the unit. Covered by unit tests that switch the flags on.
61. **Debug hooks exist only with `?debug=1`** (`src/app/debug.ts`, `window.__TH`). They are installed with `Reflect.set` and not declared as a global, because a dev page declares `Window.__TH` with another type. `state()` returns a compact summary (counts per side and kind, HQ health, queue, order, timer); `forceWeather`, `forceThunder` and `weatherStats` call the BattleView API. Playwright tests read them through a local `Hooked` type.
62. **The HUD and menu stylesheets used the same class names, which broke the campaign screen once both were loaded** (found by the browser smoke test: the HUD's `.th-card{display:none}` hid the mission card). The HUD classes were renamed: `th-card` to `th-ctrl-card`, `th-btn` to `th-hbtn`, `th-plate` to `th-hplate`, `th-map` to `th-hmap`, `th-bar` to `th-hbar`, `th-orders` to `th-horders` (in `src/ui/hud/hud.css` and `index.ts`). A scan of classes, keyframes and custom properties finds no other overlap between `hud.css`, `menus.css` and `theme.css`.
63. **Sounds of the battlefield belong to the renderer** (`view.setAudio(audio)`: shots, blasts, deliveries, trained units, order whistles, rain, thunder, engines). The app plays only the victory and defeat stings (`src/app/sfx.ts`), calls `setListener` every frame and drives `view.setDarkness` from a deadline timer (night falls as it runs down). If the renderer ever plays the stings too, delete `src/app/sfx.ts`.
64. **The HUD module is loaded with a dynamic import in `src/main.ts` before the UI is created,** and passed to `createUi` as `createHud`, so there is no frame where a battle has no HUD. The page shows a Loading overlay (`#boot`) until the title is up, and has an inline favicon so the browser never requests `/favicon.ico` (a 404 would fail the smoke test's no-console-errors rule).
65. **Quartermaster upgrades are read from `src/data/upgrades.ts` (export `UPGRADES`) through `import.meta.glob` in `src/app/upgrades.ts`;** with the file missing (Phase 1) the Quartermaster is simply empty. The level text (`+10% health`) is generated from the effects (`src/app/campaignView.ts`).

Effects, weather and sound (RENDER part 2)
66. **Phaser picks a texture in the fragment shader with an exact float compare (`outTexDatum == 7.0`), and on rotated quads that fails on some pixels, which then draw nothing (holes and wedges in big rotated sprites, found with the explosion fireballs).** It only goes wrong for texture units above 0, and a batch numbers its textures in the order they first appear, so the backdrop textures (first in a frame) pushed the atlas to unit 7. Fix in `view.ts`: an invisible speck from the atlas is the first child of the `sky` and `foreground` layers, which makes the atlas unit 0 in every batch that starts there. Rule for new code: do not put anything that flushes the sprite batch in the middle of the frame (a Phaser Graphics object, for one), which is why the lightning bolt is drawn from stretched sprites (`src/render/weather/bolt.ts`). Not seen on a real phone GPU; SwiftShader only.
67. **Layers added for the effects:** `layers.lit` (fireballs and tracers) and `layers.glow` (all additive flashes, sparks, the HQ and pillbox gun slits) sit just above `DEPTH.nightTint`, so muzzle flashes and explosions stay bright against the night tint; `layers.grade` sits between the backdrop and the ground and holds the rain's sky grade. The existing `DEPTH` table is unchanged.
68. **Effects are driven by `EFFECT_HANDLERS` (one function per sim event) plus a state-driven `Ambient` pass** (footstep and track dust, tank exhausts, headquarters smoke and fire from its health, smoke-screen clouds, tank wrecks that burn). Extra particle presets are in `src/data/renderFx.ts`, tunables in `RENDER.fx`, `RENDER.night`, `RENDER.weather` and `RENDER.audio`. A hit or miss shows when its tracer arrives (a small delayed-spawn queue). Dust, casings, exhaust and the like thin out when the pool passes `softParticleCap` or the smoothed real frame time passes `slowFrame`, and stop at `stopFrame`.
69. **Projectiles are drawn from `projectile` events** (arc by delivery: lob, direct, or a bomb dropped from the aircraft; flight time from `event.value`), with the explosion left to the `explosion` event. Because the phase 1 sim resolves every weapon as an instant `shot`, a `shot` of a lobbed or direct weapon also draws a short provisional flight that ends in a blast of the weapon's splash size; a `projectile` event in the same step cancels it, so the phase 2 sim can emit both without doubling. Ready for the phase 2 events: `projectile`, `explosion`, `supportCalled`, `supportIncoming`, `smokeSpawned`, `wireDestroyed`, `structureDestroyed`, `battleEnded` (all verified with injected events in `dev/render-preview.html`, `window.__TH.emit`).
70. **Weather is decided on `attach`** from a view-only random source seeded by `weatherRng(visualSeed)` (`src/render/weather/rng.ts`; its first draw is the 40% rain roll from `src/data/weather.ts`; the thunder checks use the same stream). `weather.update(dt)` gets the `dtSeconds` given to `render()` (battle seconds, 0 while paused), so there is exactly one check per 2 s of unpaused battle time (checked in the browser: 1.9 s gives 0, 2.0 s gives 1, 9.3 s gives 4, 20 s in 16 ms frames gives 10, 2000 s gives 1000 checks and 5 strikes) and it keeps counting while effects are off (fast-forward) even though streaks and flashes are then hidden. `forceWeather('rain' | 'clear')` makes a new controller, so the counters restart. The rain data's streak counts are scaled by `RENDER.weather.density` (1.4) and the flash opacity by `flashGain` (1.35), after looking at screenshots.
71. **Rain grade:** the sky and backdrop get the flat `skyTint` at `skyDarken` from the weather data (layer `grade`); the whole scene, units included, gets the theatre's own `rain.darken` and `rain.desaturate` as one grey overlay (alpha and colour worked out so it equals a grey pull plus a darkening), both fading in with the rain.
72. **Night tint:** `setDarkness(d)` moves the tint's alpha from the mission's own time of day toward `RENDER.night.maxAlpha` (0.52) in proportion, 0 leaving it as before. Muzzle flashes and blasts add a soft glow when the scene is dark (`ctx.night` above 0.15).
73. **Sound director (`src/render/effects/sound.ts`):** it takes any `AudioSink` (a `GameAudio` fits), plays nothing without one, and limits plays in real time: a minimum gap per sound name, 70 plays per second, nothing more than 1.2 screens off. It also calls `setListener` when the camera moves (the app need not), runs the rain loop while it rains, and runs the `tankEngine` loop while a tank is drawn (louder up to 3 tanks, stops 0.8 s after the last). Impact ricochets and dirt thuds play only for a share of impacts (`impactChance`).
74. **Screen shake moves the camera and adds just enough zoom (a few percent at the strongest) that the view never leaves the picture,** so no black edge shows at the map ends or the top and bottom. Strength falls off with the blast's distance from the screen.
75. **At the end of a battle the sim stops stepping, so unit speeds and actions are stale: the view now draws every unit standing (speed 0, idle) once `state.over`, and the winners cheer.** The losing headquarters collapses (chain of blasts, dust, debris, smoke and flames that die down after 25 s) when it is destroyed, and also when the battle was lost on a timer with the HQ still standing (the ruin sprite is forced).
76. **The "+N" supply text is drawn from a small glyph atlas** (digits and plus, drawn with Canvas text at start-up, `rd/glyph/*` in `src/render/sprites.ts`) with pooled images, so there is no per-frame text object or texture.

Phase 1 verification

Unit-tested in `tests/unit/app-debug.test.ts`.
78. **Browser tests share `tests/e2e/helpers.ts`** (the fixture that fails on any console error, page error, failed request or HTTP error, plus the debug wrappers, a touch drag and the high-speed battle player). The pan test now uses a finger (Chrome DevTools touch events) as well as a mouse. Every mission in `MISSIONS` gets the same run: start, train, give all three orders, pan, fast-forward to a result screen; today that is mission 1 only. The suite also checks sound unlock, weather rolling, HUD values against the sim, a drawn minimap, a hidden tab, all three difficulties, and the settings, reset question and Quartermaster screens.
79. **`tests/e2e/phase1-shots.spec.ts` writes `docs/screenshots/phase1-*.png`** (title, campaign, briefing, battle start, sappers hauling, firefight, fall back, HQ attack, victory, defeat) at one device pixel per CSS pixel. The fight scenes restart mission 1 on a fixed seed with a 9-rifleman army, because a larger army makes the AI shelter in its HQ and then there is no firefight in the open. The HQ-attack frame is taken by freezing the loop (time scale 0) just after a shot lands, so the muzzle flash and tracer are on screen.
80. **A win can show "Kills 0":** the AI orders everyone into its HQ when it is outnumbered, and units inside cannot be hit, so a big army destroys the HQ without a kill. The sim counts kills correctly (a passive player's HQ gun scores 11 on the defeat screen), so nothing was changed; revisit with the AI tuning in Phase 4.

Phase 1 visual QA
81. **`tools/qa.ts` and `window.__TH.units()` for visual QA.** The tool plays mission 1 in the production build under a Playwright fake clock (`page.clock`), so film-strip frames are exactly 50 ms of game time apart; it writes full-screen scenes and cropped, enlarged strips (running, carrying, loading, door in and out, firing, a death, idle, cheer, a lightning flash) to `.tools/shots/qa` at any viewport. `units()` lists every living unit (id, kind, side, action, x, row, hp, carrying, inside) so a script can follow one soldier; unit-tested in `app-debug.test.ts`.
82. **Packed units are spread on screen, view only.** Depth rows moved from +-21 to +-24 wu (still inside the trench lip at -30, the parapet berm at 52 and the wire strands at -27 and 28), each row gets a fixed x stagger (`RENDER.unit.rowStagger`) on top of a smaller random jitter, and figures of one side on one row that stand closer than `crowdGap` are relaxed apart by up to `crowdMax` wu. The crowd offset only changes while the unit is walking, so nobody slides on the spot, and the gait reads the drawn distance, so feet stay planted. The sim positions are untouched; tracers, picking and deaths use the drawn position.
83. **HQ doorway.** Within `doorFunnel` wu of its own door a unit's row offset and spread ease to zero, so men file out of the doorway and spread to their rows, and gather into single file to go back in. In the doorway a figure is tinted to a black silhouette (`doorShade`) and the alpha fade is short (`doorFade` 14), and the light rim fades as alpha cubed, so a half-faded soldier is a dark shape melting into the dark door instead of a see-through outline drawing. The training puff now rises from the feet, not across the chest.
84. **Bodies lie under the living.** When a unit dies its images move to the back of its row's layer; a new unit's images are brought to the front, so a reused pool view is never hidden under an older body.
85. **Personal carry style.** Each soldier's weapon on the move gets a seeded extra tilt (up to 0.16 rad) and height (up to 1.2 wu) (`FIGURE.gait.carryTiltVar`, `carryLiftVar`), so a running squad is not a hedge of identical parallel rifles. Hands stay pinned to the weapon.

Phase 2 sim, part 1 (fortifications, projectiles, full roster)
86. **Splash hits enemy units and enemy structures only, plus wire of either side** (as the task brief says; DESIGN 6 said "both sides' structures"). Distance is measured from the edge of each target's footprint (`unit.width`, `config.pillboxWidth`, `config.hqWidth`), so a round on a tank's hull does full damage; falloff is linear to `splashRadius`. Splash damage goes through the deferred damage queue.
87. **Direct and lobbed rounds both land at the aim point plus Gaussian scatter** of `weapon.scatter x max(scatterMinFrac, distance / range)`; direct rounds simply fly flat and fast. Nothing is hit on the way. Every such shot still emits `shot` (hit false) and, in the same step, `projectile`; the renderer already pairs them.
88. **Tanks crush only enemy wire**, so a side's own tanks do not wreck its defences. Wire is hurt only by explosive splash (rockets are antiTank and do not cut it).
89. **Trench rules.** Hold: trench-capable infantry take the nearest free slot of the hold-line trench; a man already sitting in a rear trench moves up only when a slot there is free; everyone else forms up behind the trench's rear edge (`config.trenchFormGap`). An occupied trench is a blocker to its far edge, so the other side never stands in it. A trench changes hands when one side's infantry (sappers excepted) stand inside it and the other side's do not; both sides are counted before any change, so pool order never decides. Garrisons placed by the mission (`trenchId`) take their trench without a capture event.
90. **Guards (boss tank) fight from their post and, when hit while nothing is in range, roll toward the enemy for `config.guardAlertTime`, never further than `guardLeash` from the post.** A guard with a target never moves, which keeps the existing "stops at firing range from a guard tank" test true.
91. **New weapon and unit fields are required, not optional** (`setupTime`, `dugIn`, `preferStructures`, `thrown`, `followsLine`): with optional fields the weapon objects had five hidden-class shapes and every hot-loop read of a fractional field allocated a heap number (the 80-unit allocation test went from 25 to 1250 bytes a step).
92. **Crew weapons set up after moving** (`setupTime`: lmg 1 s, mortar 1.2 s, action `deploy`) before the aim starts; a unit placed standing starts set up. Grenades (`thrown`) are released `config.throwReleaseTime` (0.35 s) into a `throw` action of `config.throwPoseTime` (0.8 s), only between bursts of the SMG.
93. **Target ranks.** Area weapons (`dugIn`): mortar `prefer`, grenade `only` (a lone man in the open is never grenaded); dug-in = in a trench, in a cluster of `config.clusterMin` (3) within `clusterRadius` (50), or a pillbox. Rockets `preferStructures`: tanks, then pillboxes and the HQ, then infantry.
94. **Medics and mortars `followsLine`**: on Advance they stay `formationDepth` behind the leading line unit and never count as the line. Medics treat only men who are standing still (chasing a moving patient made them stutter between walk and heal every tick). A standing unit sets off again only when its goal is more than `config.moveDeadband` (14 wu) away.
95. **Tank `engageFrac` 0.85 -> 0.62** (heavy tank too): tanks now close to about 260 wu, inside their coaxial MG's reach, instead of shelling from 357 wu where anti-tank rockets (300 wu) could never answer. With this, four anti-tank teams beat a tank of the same cost and a tank beats nine riflemen (tested).
96. **AI: at the population cap an Advance is not called off for a low strength ratio**, only when the attack is broken (`minAttackStrength x regroupFrac`). Before, a capped AI flipped Advance/Hold every 12 s and sandbox battles never fought. Mission 1 is unchanged (bot beats AI 10 of 10 in about 3.1 min).
97. **Sandbox grounds** `SANDBOX` (id 901) and `SANDBOX_MIRROR` (id 902) in `src/data/missions/sandbox.ts`, not in `MISSIONS`; `tools/sim-cli.ts --mission sandbox | sandbox-mirror`. Mirrored AI vs AI there: 8 / 10 / 2 undecided over 20 seeds.
98. **Bigger battlefield (view only).** The camera shows `VIEW.viewHeightWu` 410 wu (was 540) with its bottom edge at `VIEW.viewBottomWu` 524 (`VIEW_TOP_WU` 114): a figure is about 55 CSS px tall on a 390 px screen instead of 40. The crop is upper sky plus 16 wu of foreground grass that sits under the HUD anyway, chosen (over a pure bottom anchor) so both HQ flags clear the HUD objective banner at 667x375. Camera maths, picking, `unitScreenPos`, shake, the minimap window (from `viewWidthWu`) and `tools/qa.ts` all follow it. 450 and 410 were compared at 844x390 and 667x375.
99. **Camera edge margin** `RENDER.camera.edgeMargin` 64 wu past each map end, and a battle opens at the player's end against that margin, so the whole HQ, its flag and gun slit are clear of the screen edge and of a phone notch.
100. **Turning round** takes `RENDER.unit.turnTime` 0.16 s (tanks `tankTurnTime` 0.7 s): the drawn facing sweeps cos(pi t) from +1 to -1 and every sprite is squashed along x keeping its own length (joints stay joined) and thickness, never narrower than `turnMinK`. The sim facing still flips in one tick; muzzles follow the drawn facing.
101. **Firing lines read as men**: one crowd lane per side across all rows; packed men open out behind the leading man (`crowdGap` 15 wu, at most `crowdMax` 80), never toward the enemy, so the front of a line stays where the sim has it. A standing man more than `crowdDeadband` 5 wu from his place steps aside at `crowdShuffle` 30 wu/s with walking legs (the gait is fed the drawn motion). 107. **Direct control** (`src/sim/control.ts`): `control` takes a unit by its unique id (not the pool index); one per side, taking another releases the first; refused for sappers, enemies, garrisoned units and unknown ids. The unit ignores orders (Fall Back included), walks at `speed x |moveX|` under the front clamp and wire slow, keeps its target and settles its aim even on the move (crew weapons still set up) and shoots only while `fire` is held (`MountState.trigger`). `ability` is a one-tick press that throws/fires the secondary at once if it has a target and is ready; under control a grenade is never thrown otherwise, the coaxial MG keeps firing by itself. Standing still inside a trench that is not enemy-held it claims the nearest free slot and steps onto it. Release and battle end clear control without an event; death emits `controlLost`.
108. **Supports** (`src/sim/supports.ts`): per side and kind, cooldown starts on the call; the first round leaves `delay` s later, the rest evenly over `duration`. Events: `supportCalled` on the call (value = delay, dir = caller's facing) and `supportIncoming` `warning` s before the first round (value = time left; on the call itself when warning >= delay). Artillery lands at random within x +- spread from `launchOffset` behind; the air strike sweeps the strip in the caller's direction. New `SupportDef` fields `warning`, `launchOffset`, `hqDamageMul` (0.25: a barrage cannot replace an army against an HQ), `vehicleDamageMul` (air strike 3: its anti-tank punch on explosive rockets), `pattern`.

A unit already standing to fight no longer re-decides (and steps forward) on every new target.
